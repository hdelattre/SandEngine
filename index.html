Help
Draw: left mouse / touch
Erase: right mouse / two-finger (or hold Shift)
Pick: middle-click (or Alt)
Paste Image: Ctrl/Cmd+V (Shift = no scale)
Steps: simulation ticks per frame (higher = faster)
Hotkeys: Space pause • 1–0, -, = select • I ice • S salt • B brine • W wire • E spark • V battery • [ ] brush • T temp view
